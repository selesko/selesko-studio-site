Mahlum Portland Studio

A workplace shaped by healthy materials and local craft.

Project

A workplace shaped around healthy materials, adaptable working patterns and connection to the community. The tenant improvement reuses an industrial building, with exposed timber structure and daylight anchoring the studio.

Contribution

Jeff's work included staff outreach, project design, documentation and construction administration. He collaborated with local fabricators on an adjustable benching system, developing iterations and physical mock-ups for staff to test.

Material research supported the Living Building Challenge requirements. Salvaged floorboards from Fort Vancouver became vestibule cladding, while local artists created ceramic vessels for the studio's plants. A flexible community room supports gathering, presentations and everyday use.
